Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information THE PARADOX (the "Game"), developed by joe developments ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Epic Account information (online play only). If you choose to sign in with Epic Games to use online lobbies, Epic Account Services shares your Basic Profile with the Game: your Epic account ID and display name. We do not request your friends list, email address, or presence.
  • Multiplayer session data. While you host or join an online match, your display name, a session/lobby identifier and network connection information are shared with Epic Online Services and with the other players in that match so the match can function.
  • Local game data. Campaign progress, best times and settings (volume, mouse sensitivity, graphics options) are stored only on your own computer. They are not sent to us.

We do not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. The Game contains no advertising and no in-game purchases.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in, create and list lobbies, and connect players in online matches.
  • To show your display name to other players in the same match.
  • To save your campaign progress and settings on your device.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Third-party services

Online features are provided by Epic Online Services and Epic Account Services, operated by Epic Games, Inc. Their processing of your data is governed by the Epic Games Privacy Policy. You can play the single-player campaign without signing in.

4. Retention

We do not operate our own servers and do not keep a database of players. Lobby and session information exists only for the duration of a match within Epic Online Services. Local game data remains on your computer until you delete it or uninstall the Game.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can play offline without signing in to Epic.
  • You can remove THE PARADOX's access to your Epic account at any time from your Epic Games account settings under Apps and Accounts.
  • To delete local data, delete the TheParadox\Saved folder inside the Game's installation folder (and %LOCALAPPDATA%\TheParadox if present), or uninstall the Game.
  • For privacy questions, or to request access to or deletion of any data associated with your account, email youssef.ahmed4010@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through online play, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Sign-in is handled by Epic Games; the Game never sees your Epic password.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
