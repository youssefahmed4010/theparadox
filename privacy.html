Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information THE PARADOX (the "Game"), developed by joe developments ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous online device identifier (online play only). If you choose to use online arenas, the Game creates an anonymous Device ID with Epic Online Services (Connect) so you can host and join lobbies without creating or signing in to an Epic Games account. We do not collect your email address, real name, friends list, or account password.
  • Multiplayer session data. While you host or join an online match, the Contestant Name you choose, a lobby identifier, and peer-to-peer network connection data are processed by Epic Online Services and shared with players in that match so the match can function.
  • Local game data. Campaign progress, best times, chosen Contestant Name, and settings (volume, mouse sensitivity, graphics options) are stored only on your own computer. They are not sent to us.

We do not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. The Game contains no advertising and no in-game purchases.

2. How we use information

  • To connect players, create and list lobbies, and route peer-to-peer multiplayer traffic.
  • To show your chosen Contestant Name to other players in the same lobby or match.
  • To save your campaign progress and settings locally on your device.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Third-party services

Online multiplayer lobbies and peer-to-peer relay services are provided by Epic Online Services, operated by Epic Games, Inc. Their processing of network and Device ID data is governed by the Epic Games Privacy Policy. You can play the entire 30-chapter campaign offline without connecting to Epic Online Services.

4. Retention

We do not operate our own servers and do not keep a database of players. Lobby and session information exists only for the duration of a match within Epic Online Services. Local game data remains on your computer until you delete it or uninstall the Game.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can play the single-player campaign or LAN/direct-IP matches completely offline without using Epic Online Services.
  • To remove your local Device ID credential on Windows, delete the saved EOS credential in Windows Credential Manager (or uninstall the Game).
  • To delete local saves and settings, delete the TheParadox\Saved folder inside the Game's installation folder (and %LOCALAPPDATA%\TheParadox if present).
  • For privacy questions, or to request access to or deletion of any data associated with your online ID, email youssef.ahmed4010@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through online play, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Online lobbies use Epic Online Services' encrypted peer-to-peer transport and anonymous Device ID tokens; the Game never asks for passwords.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
