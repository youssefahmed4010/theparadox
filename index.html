THE PARADOX

Axiom Institute has sealed you inside an experiment. A wall is only a decision. Shift between two realities, read what each one hides, and decide whether the exit is real.

Reality Switch

Change the world between Stable and Fractured states. Routes open in one reality and close in the other.

30-Chapter Campaign

Six sectors — The Awakening, The Fracture, The Machine, The Deception, The Loop and The Architect.

Echoes

Record a previous self and let it hold a pressure plate while you move on.

Eight-Player Arenas

Race other contestants through shared bridges and shared consequences using Epic Online Services peer-to-peer lobbies — no Epic account required.
